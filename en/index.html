ABOUT

Not a simple relaxation massage.

Stress Relieve® is a hands-on salon technique that combines myofascial release and sports stretching, designed around how the whole body is connected.

Rather than simply “flushing” the body, you learn to read fascia, mobility and the links between areas, then build each treatment so clients can genuinely feel the difference before and after.

Developed in Japan by Amy and refined through more than 30,000 clients, it is a technique made for real salon work.

Myofascial release Shoulder-blade care Sports stretching Fully hands-on Preventive wellness
WHAT YOU LEARN

More than a sequence.

Anyone can memorize the order of movements. What makes Stress Relieve® work is everything in between, and that is exactly what you learn in Japan.

Body mechanics

Use your own body weight and posture, not just your hands, so the work stays effective and sustainable.

Hand placement

Exactly where your hands go, and why, for every part of the treatment.

Pressure direction

The angle and direction of pressure that let you work with fascia instead of against it.

Breathing

How to time your breathing, and your client’s, so each stretch goes deeper with less force.

Transitions

Seamless links between movements, so the treatment flows as one continuous experience.

Client assessment

How to read each client’s condition and adapt the treatment to the body in front of you.

Amy teaches and corrects your technique directly, adjusting your hands, your angle and your pressure until it is right.
